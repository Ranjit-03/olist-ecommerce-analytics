# Olist E-Commerce Sales & Delivery Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyses the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset to understand sales performance, delivery reliability, payment behaviour and geographic business performance.

The objective was to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights using SQL and Power BI.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse monthly and category-wise sales performance.
- Evaluate delivery reliability and its relationship to customer review scores.
- Understand customer payment methods and installment behaviour.
- Compare revenue, average order value and delivery performance across Brazilian states.

## Tools and Technologies

- **SQL – MySQL Workbench:** Data exploration, querying and business metric calculations.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **Power BI Desktop:** Data modelling, DAX measures and interactive dashboard development.

## Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report contains four main analytical pages:

1. **Sales Overview** – Delivered product revenue, order volume, average order value and monthly and category-wise sales performance.
2. **Delivery Performance** – Delivery time, delayed orders, delivery reliability and customer review scores.
3. **Payment Analysis** – Payment methods, payment value and installment behaviour.
4. **Geographic Analysis** – State-wise revenue, average order value and delivery performance.

Supporting pages provide a deeper look at selected delivery and installment findings.

## Key Findings

- Delivered product revenue was approximately **R$13.22 million** across **96,478 delivered orders**.
- Average order value was **R$137.04**.
- Health & Beauty was the highest revenue-generating product category, contributing approximately **R$1.23 million**.
- **93.23%** of delivered orders with valid delivery dates arrived on or before the estimated delivery date.
- Delayed orders had a lower average review score (**2.27**) than early-delivered orders (**4.29**).
- Credit cards accounted for **78.34%** of total payment value.
- São Paulo generated the highest delivered product revenue among Brazilian states, at approximately **R$5.07 million**.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor monthly order volume, revenue and average order value to identify changes in sales performance.
- Investigate delayed orders and monitor delivery reliability by month and state.
- Continue supporting commonly used payment methods, particularly credit cards and boleto.
- Compare state-wise sales and delivery performance to identify areas for further business investigation.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of SQL and Power BI to analyse e-commerce data, develop interactive dashboards and communicate business insights. It provided practical experience in data exploration, data preparation, data modelling, metric calculation and business-focused analysis.
